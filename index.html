Setup & playback
  1. Add a YouTube video/live URL or a direct HLS, DASH, or video URL under Edit source.
  2. Choose Open TV window, then click in that window if your browser asks for playback permission.
  3. Choose Listen for one stream, or Mute all. You can move the TV window to another monitor or cast it using your browser.

Website pages and videos that block embedding cannot play here. Direct streams need compatible codecs and cross-origin permissions.
